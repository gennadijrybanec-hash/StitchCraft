# Privacy Policy for StitchCraft

Last updated: August 24, 2026

StitchCraft is an application for creating cross-stitch patterns from images.

## Data collection

StitchCraft does not collect, store, or transmit personal information of users.

The application does not require user registration and does not collect:
- name;
- email address;
- contacts;
- location data;
- photos or images uploaded by users;
- usage statistics.

## Images and projects

Images selected by the user are processed locally on the device.
Project files are stored locally on the user's device.
StitchCraft does not upload user images or projects to any server.

## External material search

StitchCraft can open the device browser to search for cross-stitch materials such as DMC floss and Aida fabric. The app does not send the user's source image or project file to stores. The external website or search provider may process data according to its own privacy policy.

## Third-party services

StitchCraft does not share user data with third parties.

## Children's privacy

StitchCraft does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
